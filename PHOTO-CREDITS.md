# Photographs in Southbank Ready

Retrieved 12 October 2026. These are historical photographs, not live flood conditions. None of the photographers endorses this module. Preserve these credits and the Creative Commons licence links when distributing the photographs.

The JPEG files are bundled for offline use. No local image edits were made. Two files are smaller versions generated by Wikimedia Commons; all images retain their full composition and proportions. The surrounding illustrations in `dist/visuals.js` are original learning aids, not photographs or maps of actual building infrastructure.
